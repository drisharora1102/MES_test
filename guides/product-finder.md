# Product finder: how to narrow a vague request to a product

A starting guide. Senior reps should correct and extend it: it is plain text, and the assistant follows whatever it says.

## How to run it

- When a request doesn't name a product, find out what the customer needs before recommending. Ask one question per message, the one that rules out the most products. Usually two to four questions are enough.
- Skip any question the person has already answered, even indirectly ("old metal halides in the warehouse" answers the space and what's there now).
- If the person doesn't know an answer, assume the common case, say what you assumed, and move on.
- Then recommend one to three specific products from our catalog, each with its link and one line on why it fits. Say what to confirm before quoting, such as the wattage or lumen package.

## First question: what is the space?

Warehouse or manufacturing · Gym or other high ceiling · Office or classroom · Retail or grocery · Parking lot or area · Building exterior · Gas station or canopy · Parking garage · Cooler or freezer · Something else

## Then, by space

**High ceilings (warehouse, manufacturing, gym)**
1. Mounting height. Above about 20 ft usually means a high bay; lower ceilings suit low bays or linear strips.
2. Open floor or racked aisles. Racked aisles need a narrow (aisle) beam.
3. Replacing what? Metal halide and the wattage, fluorescent T5 or T8 high bays, or a new building.
4. New fixtures, or retrofit kits that reuse the existing housing.
5. Motion sensors or dimming wanted?
6. Voltage, if the building runs on 480V.

**Offices, classrooms, retail**
1. Ceiling type: drop ceiling grid (2x2 or 2x4) or hard ceiling.
2. Replacing fluorescent troffers? New fixtures (flat panel, troffer) or retrofit kits.
3. Dimming or occupancy sensors wanted?

**Parking lots and areas**
1. Pole height, and how many fixtures per pole.
2. What's there now: fixture type and wattage.
3. Replace the whole fixture, or retrofit the existing head.
4. Mounting: arm, slip fitter or tenon.
5. Photocell or motion control wanted?

**Building exterior**
1. Wall packs, flood lights or something else.
2. Mounting height and what's there now (wattage).
3. Dark-sky or full cutoff required?
4. Photocell wanted?

**Gas stations and canopies**
1. Canopy height and what's there now.
2. New fixtures or retrofit.

**Coolers and freezers**
1. Walk-in cooler, freezer or display case, and the temperature.
2. Lighting only, or energy savers too (anti-sweat heater controls, door closers, motor controls, strip curtains).

## Words customers use

- "UFO light" or "round high bay": a round LED high bay.
- "Corn cob" or "corn bulb": a screw-in LED lamp that replaces an HID bulb.
- "Shoebox": an area light for parking lots.
- "Troffer": a recessed ceiling fixture for a drop-ceiling grid.
